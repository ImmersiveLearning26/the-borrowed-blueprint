# Source fidelity, scoring and explicit decisions

Authority: `source-brief.pdf`, DPA 9109 The Borrowed Blueprint. The latest user direction replaces all older 3D/globe-map treatment with a continuous flat atlas. No claim of policy or legal authority is made by this reference implementation.

## Rubric implemented

Opening stance is unscored; AC starts at 70 and fit risk at 0. Both clamp to 0–100. Higher AC and lower fit risk are desirable. Stance 0 provides Catalonia without spending research hours. Other unopened sources cost 6 hours each; replay is free. At least two opened sources are needed to continue.

| Claim | Best support | Score for 2 / 1 / 0 relevant attachments |
|---|---|---|
| A: asymmetric arrangements can support fairness | Basque/Navarre + Andalusia | 20 / 10 / 0 |
| B: open-ended constitutional renegotiation | Constitution + Catalonia | 20 / 10 / 0 |
| C: asymmetry mainly drives nationalism | Catalonia | 5 with Catalonia; otherwise 0 |
| D: litigation as central route | Constitution + Catalonia | 15 / 8 / 0 |

Exactly two distinct opened sources are attached. AC +5 for 20 points, −8 for zero, unchanged for partial. Claim C cannot reach 10 with the source's stated attachment rule; no extra points are invented.

Mechanisms: D1 bilateral negotiation, D2 multilateral fiscal council, D3 Constitutional Court. Each correct route earns 10; each incorrect route reduces AC by 5. D1 or D2 error escalates D3. Correct Court routing in escalated D3 opens the additional enforcement choice. Article 155 versus Court enforcement does not change mechanism points; Article 155 is remembered for feature C.

| Nigerian feature | Transfer | Adapt | Reject |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A: bilateral fiscal deals | 0 | 10 | 5 |
| B: sector conferences | 10 | 5 | 0 |
| C: automatic suspension | 0 | 5 | 10 |

A feature source check costs 6 reserve hours once. A 10-point feature decision adds AC 3; a zero-point decision subtracts AC 3; partial leaves AC unchanged. Unchecked Transfer/Adapt adds fit risk 10. Unchecked C with Article 155 adds a further 5. Stance 2 plus unchecked Reject subtracts AC 3. A checked source is still playable at no cost.

Closing line gives 10 for a specific lesson and 10 for a specific Nigerian constraint; generic fourth choices earn zero. All 16 sentence combinations are valid syntactically and recorded.

Challenge targets the lowest score divided by its strand maximum (20/30/30/20). Tie order is Transfer, Asymmetry, Mechanisms, Limits. Concede adds 3 final points and removes 10 fit risk. Defend with at least three opened sources adds 5 final points and AC 5; with fewer, subtracts 5 final points and AC 8. Reframe changes no points and subtracts AC 5. Closing stance is unscored.

Final raw score is strand sum plus retreat adjustment, clamped 0–100. Bands: 85+ Brief adopted; 65–84 Adopted with revisions; 40–64 Returned; below 40 Rejected. Fit risk >=30 OR AC <50 reduces the band by one level total, even if both conditions hold. Raw score remains visible in requested notes.

| Issue | This kit's decision | Production review |
|---|---|---|
| Source specifies written closing sentence | Two selections produce a concrete 27–33-word sentence | Honours user's standing no-free-text-scoring preference; subject owner may review options |

| Weakest-strand ties beyond Transfer not specified | Stable Transfer → Asymmetry → Mechanisms → Limits | Determinism added for reproducible tests |
